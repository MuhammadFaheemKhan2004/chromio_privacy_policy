1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Chromio ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application. Chromio is designed as a powerful color tool for designers, artists, developers, and color enthusiasts.

We are committed to absolute transparency: we do not monetize your private data, we do not monitor your personal activities, and any access permissions we request are strictly limited to providing the core features you choose to use.

2. Account Information & Cloud Backup Access

To ensure you never lose your saved color palettes, projects, and custom collections when switching devices or reinstalling the app, Chromio offers secure cloud backup and synchronization:

  • Why Account Access is Needed: An account (such as Google Sign-In or email authentication) is used specifically to associate your personal color library with your profile so your data can be safely backed up and restored across devices.
  • Data Stored With Your Account: We only store basic identification information provided by authentication (e.g., your unique user ID and email) along with your saved color palettes, tags, and custom settings.
  • Strictly Confidential: Your account details and backed-up color files are confidential. They are never shared, publicly exposed, or sold to third-party data brokers or advertisers.
No Data Leakage Guarantee: Your backup data is protected with secure encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest. We do not use your backup data for profiling, tracking, or unauthorized marketing.

3. Camera Permission & Media Processing

Chromio includes interactive features that allow you to pick colors directly from real-world objects and scenes around you.

  • Real-Time Color Picking: When you activate the Camera Color Picker, Chromio requests camera permission to stream live video frames to analyze hex/RGB values in real time.
  • Strictly On-Device Processing: The camera feed is processed instantaneously in volatile local device memory. No photos, videos, or camera streams are ever recorded, saved to cloud servers, or transmitted over the internet.
  • Photo Gallery Access: If you choose to import an existing photo to extract a color palette, that image is opened and analyzed locally on your device. We never upload your personal photo library.
  • User Control: Camera permission is entirely optional. You can deny or revoke camera access at any time through your device's Settings, and you can still generate, explore, and manage color palettes manually.

4. Zero Misuse & Zero Data Risk Promise

We maintain strict ethical standards regarding your information:

  • No Data Selling or Renting: We do not sell, trade, or rent personal information to any third party under any circumstances.
  • No Unauthorized Access or Leakage: All cloud communications and database operations adhere to industry best practices to prevent unauthorized leaks, breaches, or exploits.
  • No Tracking Across Other Apps: We do not track your browsing habits, location history, or usage of other applications on your device.
  • No Misuse: Your data is used exclusively to deliver the functionality of Chromio—nothing more, nothing less.

5. Storage & Device Permissions Summary

Below is a summary of permissions requested by Chromio and why each is required:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Required only for live color sampling and instant palette generation from physical environments. Not used for recording or remote storage.
  • Internet (android.permission.INTERNET): Required for account authentication, encrypted cloud backup/sync, and serving advertisements.
  • Storage / Media: Required only when you explicitly export palettes, color cards, or design assets as images to your device's gallery or file storage.

6. Third-Party Services & Analytics / Ads

Chromio works with trusted industry-standard infrastructure providers to facilitate authentication, cloud backup, and ad delivery:

  • Cloud & Authentication (e.g., Firebase / Google Identity): Used for secure user authentication and cloud database backup. These services comply with stringent global privacy regulations, including GDPR and CCPA.
  • Google AdMob: To keep Chromio free for all creators, we display ads using Google AdMob. AdMob may utilize standard device advertising identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID) in accordance with Google Play Developer policies. Learn more in the Google Advertising & Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Services: Used for platform features, app updates, and secure billing if applicable. Consult the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Data Security & Storage Practices

We employ robust technical and organizational measures to safeguard your account credentials and backup information:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data exchanged between the app and cloud backup services is encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
  • Encrypted at Rest: Cloud databases store synced palettes and user tokens with secure storage standards.
  • Access Controls: Only authenticated requests with your credentials can access or modify your personal backups.

8. User Rights & Account / Data Deletion

You have complete ownership of your data at all times:

  • Export Data: You can export your color palettes and codes to JSON, CSS, ASE, or image formats at any time.
  • Delete Account & Backups: If you wish to delete your account and all associated cloud backups, you can do so directly from the app settings or by contacting us at faheemniazi2004@gmail.com. Upon request, all your account records and cloud data will be permanently wiped.
  • Revoke Permissions: You can revoke camera or media permissions at any time through your operating system settings.

9. Children's Privacy

Chromio is not directed to children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that personal data from a child under 13 has been collected without verifiable parental consent, we will promptly delete that data.
